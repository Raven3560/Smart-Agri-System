How we work it out

  1. Crop water use: ETc = Kc × ET₀. ET₀ comes from the weather forecast; Kc depends on the crop and growth stage (FAO-56).
  2. Soil water store: the root zone holds TAW = soil water capacity × root depth. The crop is stress-free until it uses the readily available part (RAW = p × TAW).
  3. Daily balance: starting from your last irrigation, each day's crop water use is subtracted and effective rainfall is added back.
  4. Decision: irrigate when depletion reaches RAW. If useful rain is likely within 2 days, irrigation is held to save water and electricity.
  5. Energy: the gross water depth is converted to pump hours, kWh, cost and CO₂ using your pump details.
  6. Pot plants (money plant, tulsi): a watering interval from the plant's needs and the coming temperature and humidity.
