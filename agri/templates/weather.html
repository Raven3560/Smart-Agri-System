What is ET₀?

Reference evapotranspiration (ET₀) is the water a well-watered grass surface loses to evaporation and transpiration in a day, calculated with the FAO Penman-Monteith method from temperature, humidity, wind and sunshine. Multiplying it by a crop coefficient gives your crop's daily water use.
