For a good photo
  • Photograph one leaf that shows the symptoms clearly, filling most of the frame.
  • Use daylight; avoid harsh shadows and glare.
  • Hold the camera steady so the image is sharp.
  • A plain background (paper or your palm) improves accuracy.
