Questions people ask

Is SmartAgri free?

Yes. Create an account and use every feature at no cost.

Do I need sensors or any special equipment?

No. A phone with a camera and an internet connection is enough. Soil moisture is estimated from your soil type, the date you last irrigated and the weather, instead of being measured by a sensor.

Can I scan plants without taking photos one by one?

Yes. Live scan uses your phone or laptop camera and checks what's inside the on-screen square several times a second. When you find a sick plant, tap "Save report" to get the full treatment and irrigation advice.

Which crops are covered?

The photo disease check covers {{ kb.model_crops()|length }} crops: {{ kb.model_crops()|map("crop_short")|join(", ") }}. Irrigation, fertilizer and a guide to common problems cover {{ kb.crops()|length }} crops and plants in total, including wheat, rice, bajra, jowar, ragi, arhar, moong, groundnut, brinjal, bhindi, chilli, banana, mango, coconut and tea, plus pot plants like money plant and tulsi. See the full crop guide.

How accurate is the disease check?

On 760 test photos it named the right condition 94.7% of the time, and the right answer was in its top three 99.5% of the time. It works best with a single leaf against a plain background. With busy field photos it is less accurate, and it tells you when it isn't sure instead of guessing.

What if the weather service is down or my connection drops?

SmartAgri keeps the last forecast it downloaded for your location and uses that, with a clear label saying the data is not live.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Photos are stored with your account and are not shared. Location data embedded in phone photos is removed when the photo is saved.

Can it replace an agronomist?

No. It gives you a quick, informed first answer. For a serious outbreak, talk to your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra or call the Kisan Call Centre on 1800-180-1551.
